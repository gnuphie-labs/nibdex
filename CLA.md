## How to sign

nibdex uses a simple, in-repo signature ledger — no third-party CLA service.

To sign, in the **same pull request as Your first contribution**, add one line to
the Signatures section of [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md), in a commit You
author under Your own name and email:

```
- Full Name (@github-handle) — YYYY-MM-DD — I have read and agree to the nibdex CLA (CLA.md).
```

That commit is Your signature: it is attributable to You and timestamped in the
project's git history. An automated check
([`.github/workflows/cla.yml`](.github/workflows/cla.yml)) confirms Your
`@github-handle` appears in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` before a contribution can be merged;
You sign only once. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) walks through the full flow.

By adding Your line, You confirm that You have read and agree to this Agreement
and that You are authorized to do so.
